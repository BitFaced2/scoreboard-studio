RECORDED BASKETBALL CLOCK SETUP

This layout was prepared for the 31.7-second recording supplied for testing:
2026-11-15 20-50-35.mp4, showing two adjacent basketball clock displays.
It isolates Game Clock and Shot Clock on the RIGHT-HAND display and includes
four-corner correction and per-field cleanup.

The video is not included. Copy your original capture or the prepared
basketball-camera-720p.mp4 to the studio computer separately.

1. Play the video in vMix. Enable looping if desired.
2. Send the video input over NDI. Keep the full 16:9 frame with no zoom,
   cropping, borders or overlays. This layout uses normalized coordinates.
3. In Scoreboard Studio, load basketball-clocks.scoreboard.json.
4. Select the corresponding NDI source and click Connect.
5. Review both fields, then click Start NDI outputs.
6. Add Scoreboard - Game Clock and Scoreboard - Shot Clock as NDI inputs
   in vMix and position them as layers over your scorebug.

The original camera input must stay separate from the composed scorebug to
avoid feedback. The app does not open MP4 files directly. The clock jumps
back to its starting value at each loop. This is recorded footage, not a
live game or an internal demo mode. Other recordings need their own crops.
